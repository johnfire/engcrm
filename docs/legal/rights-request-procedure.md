# Rights-request and B2B outreach procedure

Effective 2026-07-23. This procedure records the controller decisions for
EngCRM lead research, outreach and AI prioritisation. It is operational policy,
not legal advice.

## Eligible lead data and first contact

- Research only public business sources and collect only business-relevant
  contact information for a documented prospective opportunity.
- Outreach is one-to-one, context-specific and approved by a human before it
  is sent. Do not use EngCRM for bulk marketing campaigns.
- Each first-contact draft includes the public privacy-information URL and an
  opt-out instruction. The CRM immediately blocks opted-out contacts.

## Rights requests and objections

- Receive requests at `car2187bus@pm.me`.
- Match the request to the recorded business email/channel where possible. If
  that is not enough, ask only for proportionate additional verification.
- Log the request, respond within one month, and use the existing export and
  erasure workflows where applicable.
- An outreach objection is honoured immediately across all future channels.

## AI scoring and free text

- Scores prioritise a human work queue only. A human may override every score
  and approves outreach before it is sent.
- Do not record or prompt sensitive-category data in free-text fields.
- Correct or erase inaccurate contact information on a verified request.
