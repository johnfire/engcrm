## Target Sectors

### Tier 1 — Start Here (High pain, decision-maker directly reachable)

**Handwerk / Skilled Trades:** Most Betriebe run on paper, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets. Key problems: truck stock tracking, quote-to-invoice pipelines, photo documentation for Bauabnahme, customer/equipment history. Decision-maker is the Meister or Inhaber — reachable directly, no gatekeepers. HWK Schwaben represents the entire crafts community in the region.

**Small Manufacturers & Zulieferer:** Many have SAP they dramatically underutilise. Production tracking runs on whiteboards and Laufzettel. Custom parts taxonomy doesn't fit generic inventory tools. Key problems: production dashboards, quality documentation for ISO audits, tool/fixture management, predictive maintenance from sensor data.

**Logistics / Spedition:** Smaller regional carriers run on phone calls, faxes, and spreadsheets. Key problems: route optimization with Lenkzeiten constraints, fleet maintenance tracking, document OCR for CMR and Frachtbriefe, customer shipment tracking portals.

### Tier 2 — Strong Pain, Slightly Harder Access

**Food & Beverage:** Batch traceability required by EU law, often done on paper. Bakeries waste 10–20% of production daily — demand forecasting alone justifies the project cost. HACCP documentation from sensor data is a strong AI use case.

**Construction:** Companies discover they lost money on a job 3 months after completion. Real-time project costing, subcontractor coordination, equipment tracking across sites, Bautagebuch digitalization.

**Agricultural Services:** Fleet and equipment management across client sites, seasonal workforce scheduling, regulatory documentation for Pflanzenschutz.

### Tier 3 — High Value, Longer Sales Cycle

**Medical Practices & MVZ:** Patient flow optimization, integration layers between incompatible systems, appointment no-show prediction. Higher regulatory barrier (Datenschutz, medical device software regulations) — worth it for group practices and MVZs with a practice manager.

**Professional Services (Steuerberater, Anwälte, Ingenieurbüros):** Document classification and routing, time tracking integrated with billing, AI-assisted contract analysis, meeting transcript summarization, knowledge base search across case files.

### Additional Segments

**Universities & Research Groups:** Professors and research teams often need custom data processing pipelines, AI-assisted analysis tools, or automation that doesn't exist in any commercial product. Low price sensitivity for the right tool, and academic institutions have their own funding channels. DLR Augsburg is nearby and a specific target.

**Defence & Security Consulting Firms:** Bespoke analysis automation, custom dashboards, document processing, and intelligence tools. Bundeswehr Zulieferer and security consultancies in Bavaria often have niche data problems that commercial software doesn't address. Higher complexity, higher value.
