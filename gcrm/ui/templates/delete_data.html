Delete your EngCRM account or data

To request deletion of your EngCRM account and associated data, email car2187bus@pm.me from the email address associated with your account. Tell us that you want to delete your account and data. Do not send your password.

You can also use this address to request deletion of specific data without deleting your account. Please describe the data you want removed. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

What happens next

We will review your request and explain the deletion of your account information and associated CRM content. If the workspace is shared, we will confirm which data belongs to you before deleting shared records. Some records may need to be kept for legal or security reasons; we will explain any such retention in our response.

Security audit records are configured for retention for 730 days. Database backups may still contain deleted data: the configured rotation keeps seven daily, four weekly and three monthly backups. These expire separately from live data. We will explain any other retention that applies to your request.

We respond to rights requests within one month. See the privacy policy for more information.
